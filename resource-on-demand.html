01 — The challenge

Remove the bottlenecks slowing your delivery operations

Traditional hiring and onboarding timelines don’t work when your talent pipeline demands speed and certainty. Organizations struggle with resource gaps that directly impact delivery, margins, and client confidence.

02 — When to engage

When you need Resource on Demand services

Operational bottlenecks impacting delivery

  • Case backlogs and delayed literature screening affecting timelines
  • Unpredictable document or case spikes requiring immediate resource augmentation
  • Margin pressure from clients due to built-in efficiency requirements in contracts

Bench capacity gaps for strategic growth

  • Need to underwrite functional bench for upcoming large bids (ideal for clinical research organizations and service providers)
  • Require flexible capacity that scales with pipeline fluctuations

Hiring inefficiencies consuming resources

  • High turnover rate among junior and mid-level staff adversely impacting hiring timelines and training budgets
  • Long, inconsistent time-to-billability for freshers and junior talent
  • High screening effort with low predictability of candidate fitment
  • Lagging onboarding timelines due to lack of structured HR or resource management systems

Internal resource drain from recruitment activities

  • Excessive internal resources (HR, functional teams, admin) spent on boot camps and campus interviews
  • Need to reduce costs by hiring production-ready junior/entry-level talent (shift-left strategy)

Probationary hiring models

  • Looking for a model where new hires remain on partner payroll for the first 6 months (probation) before absorption and client project assignment
  • Want to evaluate talent performance before full-time commitment

Speed and certainty requirements

  • Need resources immediately without 6-12 week onboarding cycles
  • Require predictable resource availability to meet client commitments and contractual delivery milestones

03 — What you get

Pre-trained, screened, and ready to deploy talent

Our resources are pre-trained on Pharma, clinical research organization (CRO) workflows and Regulatory affairs. They are screened for production readiness and validated for functional competency. Deploy them on-site, hybrid or remotely and observe them contribute independently from day one.

1

Pre-trained, deployment-ready talent

Life sciences graduates, healthcare professionals, and MBBS-trained resources screened and trained on real workflows before deployment. We validate production readiness before the resources reach you.

2

Functional expertise across critical functions

Gain access to deployment-ready talent trained in key areas such as Pharmacovigilance, Medical Information, Medical Writing, Quality Assurance, and Regulatory Assurance. These functional specialties are essential for meeting your delivery operations and compliance needs.

3

Pre-trained on real CRO workflows

Our resources are trained on real CRO workflows. They possess hands-on experience in case processing, regulatory documentation, quality standards, SOP compliance, and navigating fast-paced environments under audit scrutiny.

4

Screened to your specific requirements

We screen candidates according to your specific functional needs, experience criteria, technical and soft skills, and any unique project requirements. You receive talent tailored to your delivery model. There’s no need for extra training or reshaping generic hires.

5

Production-readiness validated before deployment

Each resource completes rigorous competency assessments, scenario-based tests, and readiness validation before joining your team. You’re hiring professionals who have already demonstrated their ability to perform the job, so there’s no guesswork or on-the-job learning curve.

6

Flexible deployment modes

Resources can be deployed on-site, hybrid, or remotely based on your delivery requirements, security policies, and compliance needs.

7

Ramp-up and bench augmentation

Ideal for project ramp-ups, bench strengthening, backfills, and short-to-medium-term delivery requirements where speed and quality both matter.

8

Reduced time-to-productivity by up to 75%

Minimize onboarding effort and accelerate independent contribution compared to traditional hiring models. Get resources to become billable faster and reducing non-productive time.

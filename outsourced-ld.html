01 — The challenge

When you need Outsourced L&D Enablement services

Your internal L&D team is stretched thin, or doesn’t exist. Your delivery teams are overburdened with onboarding new hires and conducting refresher training. Your training documentation isn’t audit ready. And inconsistent onboarding is causing skill gaps that surface after deployment.

You need enterprise-grade training capability but you don’t want to build an L&D function from scratch.

Our Outsourced L&D Enablement service gives you structured, SOP-aligned training programs designed and delivered by experienced professionals who’ve managed teams, cleared audits, and built L&D functions in real clinical research organization (CRO) and Pharma environments.

Lack of structured L&D systems

You don’t have established training frameworks, learning management systems, or standardized processes to develop talent systematically across functions and geographies.

No comprehensive assessment and grading frameworks

You lack SOPs and systems to conduct performance assessments, competency evaluations, or maintain consistent grading during training programs.

Administrative complexity of continuous training

Managing ongoing training schedules, tracking completion, coordinating across teams, and maintaining documentation has become operationally overwhelming.

Heavy dependency on functional teams for training

Your delivery and functional leads spend significant time conducting practical training and sharing real-world scenarios instead of working on client work and strategic priorities.

Onboarding curriculum outsourcing needs

You want to fully outsource onboarding program development and delivery for newly hired resources, reducing the burden on internal teams.

Need for new-age and custom topic training

Your organization is expanding into cell and gene therapy, real-world evidence, medical devices, or other specialized areas which require training expertise you don’t have in-house.

Functional group upskilling requirements

Internal functional groups need targeted training on emerging therapeutic areas, new regulations, or evolving compliance standards beyond your current L&D capability.

02 — What you get

Training designed by professionals who’ve done the work

1

Delivered by GxP-certified professionals with team leadership and audit experience

Our L&D team includes seasoned professionals with direct experience managing delivery teams, clearing inspections, and developing training in CRO and Pharma settings. They know operational demands and compliance requirements.

2

Role-based onboarding programs

Structured onboarding designed around Pharma/CRO roles, SOPs, and real delivery expectations, not generic or templated training.

3

Continuous upskilling and refreshers

Ongoing refresher training and advanced upskilling aligned to regulatory updates, new SOPs, evolving therapeutic areas, and changing delivery needs.

4

SOP-aligned training design

Training content mapped directly to your internal SOPs, processes, quality frameworks, and compliance requirements.

5

Audit-ready documentation and reporting

Training logs, assessments, completion dashboards, and readiness reports designed to support inspections, client audits, and regulatory scrutiny.

6

Scalable training delivery without internal overhead

Deliver training across teams, geographies, and functions without expanding your internal L&D headcount, infrastructure or technology stack. We scale training capacity as your workforce grows.
